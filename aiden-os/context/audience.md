# Audience

**Who:** owners of small local service businesses, starting in San Diego: plumbing, HVAC, roofing, cleaning,
landscaping. Busy, on the phone and in trucks, skeptical of tech hype.

**What they care about:** missed calls = lost jobs, time on quotes and scheduling, cost per lead, hiring.

**What stops them using AI (ServiceTitan survey, Sept 2026):** not knowing what to use it for (43%) and
how (44%), not price (17%). So: show one concrete use, with a price and a demo.

**Topics that worked so far:** missed calls / AI receptionist (video 1), AI receptionist vs answering service.
