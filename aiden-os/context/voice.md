# How Aiden writes and talks

## Rules
- Brief, simple, straightforward. Say it in fewer words.
- Casual with friends, clean and direct for work. Switch by audience.
- Never sound like AI: no "delve", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world", "unlock", "leverage",
  no em dashes, no lists of three for rhythm, no fake hype or exclamation stacks.
- Never over-explain. One idea per sentence. Concrete numbers over adjectives.
- No fake enthusiasm. Confident, a little dry.
- First person, like a smart college student who builds things, not a salesman.

## On camera (YouTube)
- Hook in the first 10 seconds: the problem or the number, not "hey guys".
- Show, then explain. One idea per video. End with one thing to try and the free call.
