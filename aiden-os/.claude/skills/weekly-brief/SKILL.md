---
name: weekly-brief
description: Monday plan. What happened last week, what moves the north star this week, and the 3 things Aiden must do. Use when he asks "what should I do this week" or on Mondays.
---

1. Pull state from the aios MCP: `my_todos`, `system_queue`, `list_runs` (last 7 days), `costs` (7 days),
   `autopilot_status`, `pending_approvals`.
2. Read `context/goals.md`.
3. Write under 200 words: last week (what shipped, what it cost), this week's 3 must-dos for him (ranked by
   impact on the north star), what the system will do on its own, and anything waiting on his approval.
4. Hand anything the system can do to it (`add_system_task`). Keep his list to things only he can do.
