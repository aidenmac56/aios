name: onboard-me
description: Interview Aiden to fill the clone's context (who he is, his story, opinions, voice). Use first, and whenever context/me.md has open questions or he says "interview me" / "grill me".
---

Goal: after this, the clone can answer "what would Aiden say/do here?" without guessing.

1. Read every file in `context/`. List what's missing or thin (start with "Open questions" in me.md).
2. Ask ONE question at a time, most valuable first. Short, specific, conversational. Accept voice-dictated
   rambling answers. Ask a follow-up when an answer is vague ("give me an example").
   Cover, in this order unless he steers: real weekly hours and blackout weeks; his story (why AI, why
   business owners, proof he can help); 3-5 strong opinions (AI, business, money, school); people and
   creators he admires and why; how he talks (phrases he uses, how he opens/closes texts and videos, words
   he hates); what he never wants to be associated with; what a great week looks like.
3. After every 3 answers, write them into the right file (`me.md`, `voice.md`, `goals.md`, `audience.md`) in
   his words, and delete the answered open questions. Facts he said go in as facts; your reads go under a
   "Claude's read (unconfirmed)" heading.
4. Stop after ~15 minutes or when he says stop. End with: what was added, and the next 3 open questions.
