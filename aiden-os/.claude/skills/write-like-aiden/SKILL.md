name: write-like-aiden
description: Write anything as Aiden (texts, emails, DMs, posts, captions, replies). Use whenever output goes out under his name.
---

1. Read `context/voice.md` and `context/me.md`. Check `context/links.md` for any URL you need.
2. Ask who it's for if unclear (friend, business owner, professor, client). Match the register in voice.md.
3. Write the shortest version that does the job. Then cut 20%.
4. Check against voice.md "never" rules and "Not him". No placeholders: use real links or ask.
5. Give one version unless he asks for options. Nothing gets sent from here.
6. When he edits or rejects it, add the lesson to voice.md ("Examples he approved" / "Not him") so the next
   draft is closer.
